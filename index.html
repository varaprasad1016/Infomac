What you are licensing

One platform, not a map with extras bolted on.

Every part of this runs in production today for a UK haulage operator — lorries, cameras, tachographs and drivers. You are licensing working software, not a roadmap.

01

Live tracking

Positions, ignition, speed and journeys from the vehicle's own unit. Geofences, trips, mileage and the reports a transport office actually runs.

02

Dashcam video

Multi-channel live view and playback straight from the vehicle's recorder, with clip download for an insurer — over the ordinary web port, so it works on a locked-down office network.

03

Tachograph compliance

Driver hours read live from the tachograph — time to break, left today, left this week — alongside the archive of downloaded driver-card and vehicle-unit files, parsed into activities and infringements.

04

Driver's phone app

Walkaround checks with photographs, shift start and end, jobs with one-tap navigation, defects and paperwork — the office sees it the moment it happens.

05

Road disruption

Live closures and roadworks from National Highways on the map, and a warning when a lorry is approaching one on its own heading — before it is sitting in the queue.

06

The dull, necessary parts

MOT and tax dates, maintenance reminders, clean-air zone charges, customer accounts and invoicing. The things that make it a business tool rather than a demo.

07

Fully remote support plans

Ongoing support delivered entirely remotely \u2014 commissioning units, configuring cameras and tachograph links, diagnosing a vehicle that has stopped reporting, and keeping the platform patched. No site visits to schedule and nobody to put on your payroll.

How licensing works

You sell and support. We build and run.

The arrangement is deliberately simple, because the complicated version is how white-label deals go wrong.

STEP 01

Your identity

Name, colours, icon and domain. Applied across the web app, the driver app and the store listings — not a logo dropped into someone else's product.

STEP 02

Your deployment

Your own instance and database. Your customers' data never sits beside another licensee's, and you decide where it lives.

STEP 03

Your customers

You hold the relationship, set the pricing and bill them. We never appear in front of them, and we do not sell into your market.

HardwareWorks with the trackers and camera recorders already common in UK haulage — Teltonika professional units and 4G multi-channel DVRs. No hardware lock-in and no reseller margin taken on devices you buy.
DeploymentHosted instance, your domain and certificate, or deployed into infrastructure you control.
AppsProgressive web app plus native builds for the App Store and Play Store, published under your developer account in your name.
ComplianceBuilt for UK and EU drivers' hours rules: EU 561/2006 limits, driver-card and vehicle-unit downloads, infringement reporting.
SupportYou support your customers. We support you — one technical contact, not a ticket queue.
Remote support plansFully remote plans covering commissioning, device configuration, fault diagnosis and platform maintenance — monthly, with a named contact. Taken by most licensees instead of hiring for it.
What we keepThe platform's intellectual property remains with Infomac Solutions. Your brand, your customers and your data remain yours.
